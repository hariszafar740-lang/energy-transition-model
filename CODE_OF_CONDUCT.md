# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to foster an open, rigorous, and respectful engineering environment for everyone participating in this project, regardless of background, experience level, nationality, or institution.

## Standards of Behavior

- Focus technical discussions on reproducible mathematics, transparent assumptions, and verifiable code.
- Provide constructive, respectful feedback on issues and pull requests.
- Refrain from harassment, personal attacks, or dismissive conduct.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported via GitHub repository communication channels and will be reviewed fairly and promptly by the repository maintainer.
