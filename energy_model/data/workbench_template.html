03 · Module 3 · cap-and-trade duality & MACC

What a tonne of CO₂ costs when the cap binds

Relaxing the annual CO₂ cap with its KKT multiplier reproduces the Pigouvian carbon tax exactly: at the dual price the tax optimum equals the cap optimum plus the value of the allowance. The marginal abatement cost curve is the derivative of the frontier, and its right-hand limit at zero emissions is the minimum net-zero price.

07 · Module 7 · explicit mean-CVaR stochastic program

Hedging the tail instead of the average

Eight weather-and-demand regimes are solved jointly. The objective is (1−β)·E[Z] + β·CVaR95%(Z) through its Rockafellar–Uryasev epigraph, in one 1,357-variable LP with explicit sparse matrices. The dual risk kernel q* shows exactly which futures the hedge is buying, and where it hits the π/(1−α) bound.

99 · verification, provenance & limits

How to reproduce every pixel

The page is a build artifact: workbench/data/etm_workbench_data.json holds solved values, and energy_model/workbench.py renders them into this single HTML file. A CI job re-renders the committed page from the committed data and fails on any drift, so the figure you are reading cannot silently diverge from the repository.

Scope & honest limits

  1. Inputs are deterministic synthetic profiles and illustrative annualised costs in an unspecified currency; they are not metered SCADA data or investment quotes.
  2. Module 7's CVaR panel is an independent explicit-LP re-derivation on its own declared 8-regime set. It is not a numerical replica of the Module 6 run, which remains the reference implementation and CLI.
  3. The cost surface is exactly solved at 25 nodes and bilinearly interpolated between them; the reported holdout error bounds the interpolation, it does not remove it.
  4. Single-node copper-plate dispatch in M1–M4 and lossless DC power flow in M5; no AC power flow, unit commitment or integer investment.
